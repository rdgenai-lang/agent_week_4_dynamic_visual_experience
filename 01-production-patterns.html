Production Patterns

Building AI systems that stay alive in the real world

A demo running on your laptop is a beginning. Production is where things break at 3 a.m. — and where three patterns separate hobby projects from systems you can trust: observability, error recovery, and multi-agent structuring. Let's watch each one come alive, step by step.

1
Pattern One
Observability — seeing inside the black box
1 / 4 Walkthrough

Why can't we just trust the model?

An AI agent looks like a black box from the outside. Input goes in, output comes out, and when something breaks, you have no idea why. Observability is the practice of opening that box — instrumenting your agent so its inner life becomes visible.

Key ideaIf you can't see it, you can't fix it. Treat your agent like a patient: you need vital signs, not guesses.
2
Pattern Two
Error Recovery — graceful failure, not catastrophe
1 / 4 Walkthrough

The happy path is a lie

In a perfect world, every API call returns 200 OK and every model produces valid JSON. In reality, networks flake, services time out, and models occasionally hallucinate. Error recovery is the art of expecting the worst — and absorbing it without crashing.

Key ideaDon't ask "will it fail?" Ask "how will I respond when it does?"
3
Pattern Three
Multi-Agent Repo Structuring — order in a crowd
1 / 3 Walkthrough

When one file becomes a swamp

Most AI projects start as a single Python file. That works — until you have three agents, five prompts, two APIs, and nobody remembers which function does what. The first sign of trouble: you scroll forever just to find anything.

Key ideaStructure isn't bureaucracy — it's the map that lets a team find things at midnight.
